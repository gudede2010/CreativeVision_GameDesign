01 / The club

Games are made of decisions.

Creative Vision is BIHZ's hands-on game design club. We study the systems behind engaging play, then turn original ideas into demos people can actually experience.

01

Think like a designer

Look beyond the screen to rules, goals, feedback, challenge and choice.

02

Build with purpose

Start with a player feeling, then design the mechanics that make it happen.

03

Learn by shipping

Prototype, playtest, revise and publish a focused game experience.
